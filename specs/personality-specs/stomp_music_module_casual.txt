STOMP Music Preferences — Casual Module

Purpose
- A short music-preference survey based on the Short Test Of Music Preferences (STOMP).
- Captures genre tastes that can inform personalization and provide soft signals about personality.

How to answer
- For each line, pick a number 1–5:
  1 = Not at all, 2 = A little, 3 = Somewhat, 4 = Mostly, 5 = A lot
- Answer for your general music tastes (not just one favorite song).

Quick context questions (optional)
- Which 3 artists or bands are you listening to most right now? (free text)
- What music do you usually listen to while working/studying? (free text)
- What music do you prefer when relaxing or socializing? (free text)

Scoring notes (for implementers)
- Normalize each response to 0–100: norm = ((resp - 1) / 4) * 100
- Compute factor composites (see mapping spec) to get MUSIC factor scores (Mellow, Unpretentious, Sophisticated, Intense, Contemporary).
- Use pairwise or artist lists as higher-confidence signals when available.

Privacy
- Treat these preferences as personal data. Do not share raw answers externally; summarize when personalizing.
