# Delivery (formerly Lens) Module (Unified Preference Delivery)
# Scale: 1 (Strongly Disagree) to 5 (Strongly Agree)
# Scoring notes:
# - This is a single test that combines REL, CAP, COG, PER, and ENV metrics.
# - Normalize each response using ((r - 1) / 4) * 100.
# - Reverse-score items marked [R].
# - Subscales:
#   REL: DEF, PEER, CHL
#   CAP: DNS, AUD, STR
#   COG: ABS, FMT, VBS
#   PER: EMP, CND, HMR
#   ENV: AUT, BUR
#
# Research basis (construct-level, adapted wording):
# - Need for Cognition (Cacioppo and Petty).
# - Interpersonal Reactivity Index (Davis).
# - Communicator style directness constructs.
# - Humor style literature.
# - Job control/workplace autonomy and Organizational Constraints Scale framing.

## REL (Relational Dynamics and Authority)
1. I prefer an assistant that is formally polite and careful rather than blunt. [DEF]
2. I prefer an assistant that speaks to me like a peer collaborator. [PEER]
3. If my assumptions are weak, I want the assistant to challenge them directly. [CHL]
4. I value emotional validation before critique when discussing frustrating problems. [DEF]
5. I am comfortable receiving corrective feedback without social cushioning. [CHL]
6. I prefer the assistant to avoid questioning my approach unless I request critique. [R][CHL]

## CAP (Cognitive Accessibility and Processing)
7. I understand complex answers better when they are split into short sections. [STR]
8. Dense paragraphs are usually easier for me than bullet points. [R][DNS]
9. I often consume assistant responses by listening (text-to-speech or voice mode). [AUD]
10. I prefer numbered steps with clear checkpoints when solving tasks. [STR]
11. I want important warnings repeated in plain language, not implied. [DNS]
12. I prefer one compact paragraph over structured formatting in most cases. [R][STR]

## COG (Cognitive and Formatting Preferences)
13. I prefer to understand first principles before seeing implementation details. [ABS]
14. I want answers that use bullets or tables whenever possible. [FMT]
15. I prefer the minimum explanation needed to act. [R][VBS]
16. I enjoy unpacking trade-offs and theoretical reasoning before deciding. [ABS]
17. Narrative prose helps me more than structured formatting. [R][FMT]
18. I want context, caveats, and alternatives even when I ask for a quick answer. [VBS]

## PER (Persona and Interaction Boundaries)
19. Before solving the problem, I prefer brief acknowledgement of how frustrating it is. [EMP]
20. I want clear recommendations even if they sound blunt. [CND]
21. I appreciate light humor in routine interactions. [HMR]
22. I prefer neutral, purely transactional tone with no emotional language. [R][EMP]
23. Diplomatic wording is more useful to me than direct criticism. [R][CND]
24. Humor in serious troubleshooting contexts usually distracts me. [R][HMR]

## ENV (Professional Ecosystem Baseline)
25. I can usually implement workflow or tooling changes without multiple approval layers. [AUT]
26. Compliance, policy, or legacy constraints frequently limit what I can do. [BUR]
27. I can decide priorities for my work with minimal supervision. [AUT]
28. Even small changes often require lengthy sign-off processes. [BUR]
29. I mainly need advice that I can execute independently. [AUT]
30. Recommendations that assume strict governance and audit constraints are usually necessary for my context. [BUR]
