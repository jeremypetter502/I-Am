# Skills Assessment Module (O*NET Standardized Transferable Skills)
# Scale: 0 (No proficiency) to 5 (Expert, can teach others)
# Scoring notes:
# - Normalize each response with score * 20 (0..100).
# - Threshold status: >=60 results-worthy, 35-59 conditional, <35 omit.
# - Scores >=50 require all three confirmation tests for filtered export:
#   Interview Defense, Day One Autonomy, Relevance & Recency.

## Cognitive & Analysis
1. Reading Comprehension: I can quickly understand and apply complex written material.
2. Mathematics: I can use quantitative reasoning to solve real-world work problems.
3. Science: I can apply scientific principles to decisions and troubleshooting.
4. Critical Thinking: I evaluate claims and assumptions before acting.
5. Active Learning: I learn new tools or processes quickly and retain them.
6. Learning Strategies: I pick effective methods to learn difficult topics.
7. Monitoring: I track outcomes and adjust when quality drops.
8. Complex Problem Solving: I can break down and solve unclear, multi-step issues.
9. Identify Patterns: I identify patterns and trends in data or behavior.
10. Data Analysis: I analyze datasets to produce useful conclusions.
11. Systems Evaluation: I evaluate how well systems perform and where to improve.
12. Judgment & Decision Making: I make sound decisions under uncertainty.

## Communication & Interpersonal
13. Active Listening: I listen for meaning and ask clarifying questions.
14. Writing: I write clearly for different audiences.
15. Speaking: I explain ideas clearly in conversations and presentations.
16. Social Perceptiveness: I notice cues about others' reactions and needs.
17. Coordination: I coordinate my actions with others effectively.
18. Persuasion: I can influence others with credible reasoning.
19. Negotiation: I can negotiate trade-offs and agreement.
20. Instructing: I can teach skills in a way others can apply.
21. Service Orientation: I proactively support people and resolve their needs.

## Leadership & Management
22. Management of Personnel Resources: I coach, assign, and evaluate people effectively.

## Technical & Specialized
23. Troubleshooting: I diagnose causes of technical/process problems.
24. Technology Design: I design or adapt technology solutions to needs.
25. Installation: I can set up systems/equipment correctly.
26. Programming: I can write or modify software/scripts for practical tasks.
27. Quality Control Analysis: I verify output quality against standards.
28. Equipment Maintenance: I perform preventive maintenance reliably.
29. Repairing: I repair systems/equipment when faults occur.

## Business & Process
30. Operations Analysis: I analyze workflows to improve efficiency.
31. Equipment Selection: I choose tools/equipment appropriate for the job.
32. Time Management: I plan and prioritize work to meet deadlines.
33. Management of Financial Resources: I budget and allocate funds responsibly.
34. Management of Material Resources: I allocate and track materials effectively.

## Creative & Innovation
35. Creativity & Innovation: I generate and apply novel useful ideas.
