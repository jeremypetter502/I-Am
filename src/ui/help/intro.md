# What this site does

<img src="/images/ziggy-iam-intro.png" alt="IAM mascot" style="float: right; max-width: 34%; margin: 0 14px 10px 0;" />Hey there, welcome! I’m **Ziggy**, your backstage guide to getting your AI on your exact wavelength. 

Ever feel like your AI doesn't quite *get* you? We're about to change that. Together, we’re going to map out your unique digital blueprint so your AI can provide perfectly tailored, insightful responses that actually sound like they're meant for *you*.

### How We'll Do It:
* **Explore the Modules:** Dive into the personality modules below. Complete as many or as few as you like—there's no pressure. Every piece you finish helps me fine-tune how the AI understands you.
* **Automatic Saves:** Don't worry about losing your place. Your progress is saved automatically in your local browser as you answer.
* **Generate Your String:** When you’re ready, click **Generate**. I’ll assemble your scores into a compact, custom **I-AM string**. Just copy and paste that string into your AI chat, and *boom*—it instantly knows exactly who it's collaborating with.

### Your Data Stays Yours
Privacy is the headliner here. **Absolutely zero information is stored on our servers.** If you want to keep your full answers to tweak them later, you can download them as a secure JSON file and reload them right here whenever you want.

**Ready to rock? Let’s build your I-AM string!**
